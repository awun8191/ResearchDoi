# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, students, and academic writers who collect source material, converse with AI research agents, and assemble thesis-style documents inside a focused workspace.

## Product Purpose

ResearchDoi helps users search, ingest, organize, and reason over academic sources with AI assistance. Success means a user can trust the workspace state, understand what the agent is doing, and move from sources to structured writing without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, warm, and lucid. The interface should feel like a modern reading room: thoughtful and academic, but soft, approachable, and transparent rather than severe or terminal-like.

## Anti-references

Avoid harsh cyberpunk dashboards, all-caps terminal language, sharp-corner lab panels, opaque black boxes, and loading states that make users wonder whether the agent forgot the conversation.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve research continuity: session identity, source context, and agent state should remain visible and stable.
2. Make agent work legible: tool calls, sub-agent handoffs, and thinking states should be readable without overwhelming the conversation.
3. Prefer soft academic clarity: rounded glass surfaces, readable typography, and gentle hierarchy over hard-edged lab aesthetics.
4. Treat loading as communication: every waiting state should explain whether memory is loading, the agent is reasoning, or tools are running.
5. Keep scholarly output readable: long-form responses need comfortable measure, contrast, and structured markdown styling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and loading indicators that are not color-only. Motion should be brief and state-driven.
